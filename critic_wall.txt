BEHAVIOUR CRITIC - MOVE AWAY FROM WALL (escape field + governor)
2026-08-07. Play mode, no .cs touched. Raw data: critic_wall_A/B/X/S/F/G/H.txt

CONTROL LEG FIRST. My harness reproduces VerbBench check N exactly on the
same rig and start point: band 5.24-6.99 m, 2 reversals, peak 2.58 m/s
(critic_wall_X.txt, run Z_ctrl_axis). So where I report a difference below,
it is a difference, not a different harness.

================================================================
BROKEN
================================================================

--- B1. EscapeSpeed() READS THE WRONG BODY AXIS. On any build whose drive
--- direction is local +X, the damping term is dead and the ENTIRE PRE-FIX
--- PATHOLOGY COMES BACK.

SensorBus.cs:221 measures wallEscapeDeg from robot.transform.TransformDirection
(forwardLocal), where forwardLocal is the build's DRIVE direction (dDir,
BuilderManager.cs:4023). ProgramRunner.cs:694 reconstructs that direction from
self.transform.forward. Those are the same vector only when driveDir == (0,0,1).
BuilderManager.cs:3471-3473 snaps driveDir to (0,0,1) OR (1,0,0) depending on
wheel roll axis, so a chassis whose wheels roll along X gets a 90 deg frame
error in EscapeSpeed and nowhere else.

Rig: the VerbBench BODY rotated 90 deg (wheel axes 0,0,+-1). Builder accepts
it, 10 parts, DriveDirNow = (1.00, 0.00, 0.00). Standing AWAY 80%, start
(4.5, 0), 20 s. I logged the true escape-axis speed and the one the runner
computes, side by side:

  t=1.02  vTrue=+6.19 m/s   vRunner=-0.29
  t=3.06  vTrue=+5.29       vRunner= 0.00
  t=6.12  vTrue=-7.05       vRunner=-0.21
  t=9.18  vTrue=-6.08       vRunner=+0.02

vRunner is ~0 whenever vTrue is 5-7 m/s, so u = Clamp01(gov - 0) = gov: the
damping term you added at ProgramRunner.cs:679 never subtracts anything.
Result, measured over 20 s:

                  band (m)      reversals   peak (m/s)   ends at
  Z drive (ctrl)  5.24 - 6.99       2          2.58       6.97 m
  X drive         1.02 - 6.98       8          8.54       1.58 m
  what R3 measured 1.02 - 6.91    5 in 11 s     7.5      inside 1.2 m

That is the old bug, digit for digit, including finishing closer to a wall
than the 1.2 m the WALL! hat defends.

HONEST CAVEAT, and it matters: X-drive builds are ALREADY broken by something
older. RaycastWheelDrive.cs:565 WheelSideX returns localAnchor.x and
ProgramRunner.cs:132 buckets left/right off it, so on an X-drive build 'left'
is the rear pair. I ran the pre-existing paths on the same rig as a control:
  S5b  TURN BY 90         -> dYaw = 0.22 deg in 3.5 s   (does nothing)
  S5c  MOVE TOWARD WALL   -> d 3.00 -> 3.01 in 2.5 s    (does nothing)
  S5a  plain MOVE 70%     -> moved 5.82 m               (works: base-only)
So differential steering is dead there regardless of your change. Two of my
X-drive runs show that half:
  X_axisZ  escape exactly abeam -> 20 s, wallDist pinned at 2.50, peak speed
           0.05 m/s, command 0.00. The robot never moves at all.
  X_corner escape abeam -> 20 s skating along the +Z wall, z stuck 5.50-5.54,
           wallDist band 1.05-1.50, never escapes.
Those two are the pre-existing steering bug, not yours. X_axisX (the 8.54 m/s
ping-pong) is yours, because there the escape is dead ahead/astern so base
thrust works and only the damping is missing.

Blame: ProgramRunner.cs:694. It should rotate the same vector SensorBus used:
self.transform.TransformDirection(driveDirLocal), not self.transform.forward.
Cheapest correct fix is to have SensorBus publish the escape direction in WORLD
space alongside the relative bearing and have EscapeSpeed dot against that -
then no caller can ever get the frame wrong again.

--- B2. A SHORT AWAY BURST IS 12x WEAKER WHEN THE ESCAPE IS ABEAM. The shipped
--- WallShy hat cannot clear its own trigger threshold in one burst.

RelDrive scales thrust by cos(escape bearing) (ProgramRunner.cs:722), so with
the escape 90 deg off the nose drive01 is exactly zero and the whole burst is
spent pivoting. Static field map confirms it: at (6,0), 1 m off the wall, the
most pinned a robot can be, the four wheel commands are +1.00/+1.00/-1.00/-1.00
and NET THRUST IS 0.000 (critic_wall_A.txt).

Measured: AWAY 80% held for 0.8 s from wallDist 1.50, by heading:
  escape 180 (wall dead ahead)   +2.57 m
  escape   0 (wall astern)       +1.52 m
  escape -135                    +1.23 m
  escape +135                    +0.88 m
  escape  -45                    +0.93 m
  escape  -90 (abeam)            +0.22 m   <-- 12x worse than the best case
At 25% the abeam case gains +0.12 m in 0.8 s.

RobotProgram.WallShy() (RobotProgram.cs:750) is:
   WHEN EdgeDist < 1.2 : MOVE AWAY FROM WALL 80% FOR 0.8 s
From 1.2 m with the wall abeam that burst ends at ~1.4 m, so the hat re-fires
immediately. In four real L1C1 autonomy fights the WALL! hat was the firing hat
15% / 73% / 3% / 65% of sampled frames and minimum wall clearance still reached
0.59-1.07 m. Same shape in my sequencer run S1 (85% FOR 1.0 s from 1.10 m,
wall abeam): the step spent its whole second turning and gained 0.56 m.

Given time it always works - 3.0 s of the same command gains 4.20-4.52 m at
80% from EVERY heading - so this is 'the burst length is wrong for the worst
heading', not 'the verb cannot escape'. Two ways out: give AWAY+Wall a thrust
floor while it is pivoting (Mathf.Max(0.25f, -cos) instead of -cos), or make
the preset's burst duration long enough for the worst case (>= 1.5 s).

--- B3. 'COMMANDS NOTHING ONCE IT IS CLEAR' IS TRUE ONLY WITHIN ~8 cm OF THE
--- EXACT ARENA CENTRE. Everywhere else a stationary, perfectly safe robot
--- keeps commanding full opposite lock.

The zero at ProgramRunner.cs:680 needs u <= 0.02, i.e. with the robot stopped,
gov <= 0.02, i.e. wallDist >= 6.92 m. wallDist only reaches 6.92 within 8 cm
of the centre. VerbBench check O samples exactly (0,0), where wallFieldValid
is false and the OTHER early return (line 657) fires, so the bench cannot see
this. Static map, robot held stationary, AWAY 80% armed:

   pos      wallDist   wheel commands    net
  (1,0)      6.00      +0.248 / -0.252   0.00   <- 6 m of clearance
  (2,0)      5.00      +0.498 / -0.502   0.00
  (3,0)      4.00      +0.748 / -0.752   0.00
  (0.5,0)    6.50      +0.123 / -0.127   0.00
  (0,0)      6.99       0.000            0.00   <- the only place it is quiet

Cost in the standing-order run: from (4.5,0) at 80% the robot reached 5 m of
clearance at t=1.70 s, but kept manoeuvring - a decaying spiral through
(-0.71,1.74), (-1.17,-0.93), (0.66,-0.66), (0.41,0.16) - and did not stop until
t~13 s. Eleven seconds of orbiting after the verb had already succeeded. At 15%
power it was still crossing the centre every ~5 s at t=19 s (x: 0.07 -> 1.03 ->
-0.42 -> -0.23).

Fix is a clearance deadband rather than an urgency one: return zero when
wallDist >= some WALL_SAFE (my runs say 5.0-5.5 m is where nothing is being
gained any more). That also makes the verb's contract legible to a player:
'open the range to 5 m, then stop'.

--- B4. N ROUNDS OF AWAY FROM WALL COMPLETES ZERO ROUNDS WHEN ALREADY CLEAR,
--- AND EXITS ONLY BECAUSE THE STALL WATCHDOG CATCHES IT.

  MOVE AWAY FROM WALL 80% 3 ROUNDS ; WAIT 5
  from the arena centre: 9 s of run, max wheel command 0.00 for the whole
  step, robot moved 0.08 m, the step ended at t=1.42 s - 1.22 s after entry,
  which is STALL_GRACE (1.2 s), not the rounds and not MAX_DUR.
  from wallDist 1.50: 3 rounds complete in 2.02 s, d 1.50 -> 4.66. Correct.
This is not a hang - the F8 watchdog saves it - but a program with a rounds
flavour of this verb silently burns 1.2 s doing nothing every time round the
loop. Worth either documenting or making the zero-command case finish
immediately instead of waiting out the watchdog.

================================================================
CONSTANTS: WHAT THE MEASUREMENTS SAY ABOUT WALL_CLEAR AND WALL_TOP_SPEED
================================================================

C1. YOU HAVE ONE KNOB, NOT TWO, AND ITS CURRENT VALUE IS 1.0 s^-1.

Solving u = 0 gives the exact speed at which thrust cuts out:

  v_cut = WALL_TOP_SPEED * Clamp01((ARENA_HALF - d) / (ARENA_HALF - WALL_CLEAR))

Outside WALL_CLEAR the clamp is inactive and only the RATIO
WALL_TOP_SPEED / (ARENA_HALF - WALL_CLEAR) survives. With 4.0 and 7-3=4 that
ratio is exactly 1.0 per second, so outside 3 m the rule reduces to the very
legible 'you may retreat at (7 - wallDist) metres per second' - i.e. you are
allowed to close the remaining gap to the centre in one second.

Verified against the trace, not assumed:
  d=5.78, escape-axis speed 1.26, predicted cut at 1.22 -> command was 0.000
  d=5.27, escape-axis speed 0.46, predicted u=0.3175 -> predicted base 0.246,
          measured net command 0.24
Changing either constant alone changes that ratio. If you want the cap and the
ramp to be tunable separately, they need to be two independent constants; right
now WALL_TOP_SPEED is the ramp slope wearing a speed-limit costume.

C2. WALL_TOP_SPEED = 4.0 IS NEVER THE BINDING LIMIT ON THIS RIG. It only acts
as a flat cap inside WALL_CLEAR, where gov saturates at 1. Peak speed across
every 80% run I did - axis, both diagonals, both corner headings - was
  2.58 / 3.79 / 3.52 / 3.52 / 3.31 / 3.27 / 3.24 m/s
Never once 4.0. So as a speed cap it is inert; nothing in my data justifies
moving it, and moving it would silently move the ramp instead.

C3. WALL_CLEAR = 3.0 IS NOT THE SET POINT. THE SET POINT IS THE ARENA CENTRE.
Every standing-order run finished at the exact centre, whatever the start:
  from (4.5,0) 80%      -> (0.02,-0.05)  wallDist 6.96
  from (5.5,5.5) yaw 0  -> (0.02, 0.06)  wallDist 6.95
  from (5.5,5.5) yaw225 -> (-0.03,-0.01) wallDist 6.96
  from (5.5,5.5) yaw 45 -> (-0.02,-0.04) wallDist 6.97
  from (5.5,0) 25%      -> (0.06, 0.00)  wallDist 6.93
  from (5.0,5.0) 25%    -> (-0.17,-0.16) wallDist 6.84
  from (5.5,0) 15%      -> (-0.23, 0.00) wallDist 6.76
The docstring's 'full authority at or inside WALL_CLEAR, tapering to nothing at
the centre' is exactly right, but a reader will take WALL_CLEAR for the target
clearance and it is not - it is only the knee of the ramp. Nothing I measured
says 3.0 is the wrong knee. What IS wrong is that there is no stopping
clearance at all (see B3): WALL_CLEAR sets where the ramp starts, and nothing
sets where the verb is satisfied.

C4. TIGHT-BOX LADDER RUNGS: I EXPECTED A PROBLEM AND DID NOT FIND ONE.
Progression.cs:66-69 ships rungs at arenaHalf 5.5 and 5.0. Since WALL_CLEAR is
absolute, span shrinks to 2.5 and 2.0 and the ratio rises to 1.6 and 2.0 - I
predicted the retreat would be up to twice as aggressive in the smaller box.
Measured, same 1.50 m clearance in each box, standing AWAY 80%, 15 s:
  half 7.0 (ratio 1.00)  peak 3.31 m/s  band 5.81-7.00  2 reversals  ends centre
  half 5.5 (ratio 1.60)  peak 3.27 m/s  band 4.46-5.49  2 reversals  ends centre
  half 5.0 (ratio 2.00)  peak 3.24 m/s  band 4.00-4.99  2 reversals  ends centre
No measurable difference, because at 1.50 m clearance gov is saturated at 1 in
all three boxes and the ratio never applies. The prediction was wrong; the
tight boxes are fine. One residual note, untested because no rung reaches it:
if a future arena ever used arenaHalf <= 3.0, span clamps to 0.01 and
WallUrgency becomes a step function - the governor would effectively switch off
and B1's pathology would return for every build. A Mathf.Min(WALL_CLEAR,
half*0.5f) guard would cost nothing.

VERIFIED CORRECT

V1. CORNERS AND DIAGONALS ARE FINE, AND THE FIELD IS A TRUE GRADIENT.
The sum is separable - push = (-g(x), 0, -g(z)) with g odd and monotone - so it
is exactly the negative gradient of V = sum of 1/(half +- x) + 1/(half +- z).
One minimum, at the centre. No local minima, no saddle, no orbit source, no
plane identity to swap. Static map at yaw 0 (escape bearing vs analytic value):
  (6,6)   -131.4  (analytic -135 at the COM's own offset)
  (6,5)   -103.9  (analytic -103.8)
  (5,6)   -164.4  (analytic -166.2)
  (6,3)    -93.0     (6,1)  -90.7     (6,0)  -90.0
Crossing the x=z diagonal is smooth: (4,3.9) -133.2 -> (4,4.0) -135.3 ->
(4,4.1) -137.4. Crossing the x=0 mid-line at z=5 is smooth: -178.6 -> 180.0 ->
+178.6, and the net command is a flat -0.800 across all three - no jump.
Corner escapes measured from (5.5,5.5): reached 3 m of clearance in 0.84-1.20 s
and 5 m in 1.64-2.50 s from all three tested headings, 1-2 command reversals,
peak 3.52-3.79 m/s, settled at the centre. Both diagonal headings drove a clean
straight line out of the corner.

V2. IT NEVER FAILED TO ESCAPE ON A NORMAL (Z-drive) BUILD. Weak retreats work:
  -25% from (5.5,0):  3 m at 1.84 s, 5 m at 2.80 s, peak 2.23 m/s
  -25% from (5.0,5.0) corner: 3 m at 1.14 s, 5 m at 2.30 s, peak 2.53 m/s
  -15% from (5.5,0) nose-away: 3 m at 1.50 s, 5 m at 2.50 s, peak 2.08 m/s
The governor plus friction never pinned it at any power I tried down to 15%.
Interesting inversion worth knowing: after 3.0 s from 1.50 m, 25% gains MORE
clearance than 80% on the two best headings (+5.30 and +5.44 vs +4.52 and
+4.32) because 80% overshoots the centre and is dragged back. High power buys a
faster first second, not more clearance.

V3. THE EQUILIBRIUM IS THE CENTRE AND IT HOLDS. 25 s run from (4.5,0) at 80%:
after t~13 s the robot sits at (0.01,-0.05), speed 0.01 m/s, command 0.00, and
stays there for the remaining 12 s. No creep, no steady orbit - the spiral
described in B3 decays rather than persisting. Same at 25% (settled by ~17 s)
and 15% (still decaying at 19 s, amplitude down from 1.03 m to 0.42 m).

V4. THE SEQUENCER INTERACTIONS ALL HOLD.

    hat fires at 0.20 s, the AWAY step runs exactly 1.00 s (0.20->1.20), TURN BY
    90 completes in 0.68 s, the hat RELEASES cleanly and hat 1 takes over at

  Timed flavour with the governor already zeroed (robot at the centre, command

    shorten or lengthen a timed step.
  3 ROUNDS near a wall completes properly (2.02 s, d 1.50 -> 4.66). The failing
    case is B4 above.

V5. THE UNCHANGED PATHS ARE UNCHANGED. All on the same rig, one step each:
  MOVE TOWARD WALL 70%   d 2.49 -> 1.17, moved 1.32 m, max cmd 0.72   closes

V6. NO CENTRE-CAMPING IN A REAL FIGHT. Four L1C1 autonomy fights with WallShy
against four with Brawler, same 14-part fixture, same contest:
                  outcome  %time within 1.5 m of centre  mean r  mean enemy dist
  WALLSHY #0..#3  4 wins   1 / 3 / 2 / 3 %               5.2-6.4  1.52-1.72 m

The feared failure mode - the retreat hat winning the fight and parking the bot

================================================================
COULD NOT REACH / DID NOT TEST
================================================================

- I could not isolate the EscapeSpeed frame bug (B1) from the pre-existing
  X-drive steering bug, because the only builds that trigger one trigger the
  other. B1 is proved by the vTrue/vRunner instrumentation, not by an isolated
  behavioural A/B.
- The fight arm is 4 fights per program, all wins, short (15-108 samples each at
  10x time scale). That is enough to rule out centre-camping and not enough to
  measure a balance shift. A proper answer wants MatrixBench's cell sizes.
- I did not re-run VerbBench itself (time); I calibrated against its check N
  numbers instead and reproduced them exactly.
- Real fights add an enemy shoving the player into walls, so the fight-arm
  minimum clearances (0.53-1.32 m, both programs) cannot be attributed to the
  verb.
- I did not test a build with the wall sensor DEAD mid-fight. Code reading says
  the branch falls through to TargetSignal, which returns false, and RelDrive
  with no signal drives straight ahead at full power - i.e. AWAY FROM WALL with
  a shot-off wall sensor drives FORWARD, possibly into the wall. Pre-existing,
  unverified, worth a look.
- Only one chassis (VerbBench BODY and the MatrixBench FIXTURE). Heavier, faster
  or higher-inertia builds will sit at a different point on the damping curve.
