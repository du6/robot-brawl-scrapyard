   The proof that commit 424d6d0 actually fixed something, rather than
   moving bytes around.

== D. what this proves that the bench could not ==
      FightWorkerBench drives a STUB transport that only counts bytes. The
      raw application/octet-stream upload over real HTTP — the exact path the
      scorecard bug lived in — is only exercised here. A ~186 KB gzip body
      went out over UnityWebRequest, through the API's content-type branch,
      into the blob store, and came back readable.
